# Video Script: Arc-Vault Demo (2.5 - 3 Minutes)
## Scene 1: Introduction (00:00 - 00:30)
**Visual:** Show the landing page of Arc-Vault.
**Action:** Click "Connect Wallet" and connect MetaMask.
**Audio:** "Hello everyone! Today I'm excited to present Arc-Vault, a revolutionary self-paying lending platform built on the Arc Testnet."

## Scene 2: Collateral Deposit (00:30 - 01:15)
**Visual:** Navigate to the Deposit section.
**Action:** Enter 1000 RWA tokens, click "Deposit", and confirm.
**Audio:** "Now, I'm going to deposit 1000 RWA tokens as collateral. By depositing them into our secure vault, we unlock borrowing power."

## Scene 3: Borrowing & Yield Generation (01:15 - 02:00)
**Visual:** Show the "Borrowed" balance increasing.
**Action:** Click "Start Self-Paying Stream".
**Audio:** "Once the collateral is locked, we can borrow USDC. The yield generated automatically starts paying off my debt in real-time."

## Scene 4: Settle & Conclusion (02:00 - 02:45)
**Visual:** Show the "Settle" button and ArcScan.
**Audio:** "At any point, I can settle the balance. All operations are fully verifiable on ArcScan. Thank you!"
